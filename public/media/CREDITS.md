# Artwork

- `nebula-blue.avif` and `nebula-violet.avif`: nebula artwork supplied by Eugen Ranow; resized and compressed. Original authors and licensing were not supplied.
- `unity-world-concept.avif`: generated environment concept artwork for the card, not a screenshot of the Unity projects.

## Apex Desktop showcase

- `apex/home.webp`, `apex/system.webp` and `apex/compression.webp`: actual Apex Desktop 1.0.0 windows captured on 2026-10-22 after the Geist and flame redesign, with live footer telemetry; losslessly encoded for web delivery. No interface elements or results were fabricated.
- `apex/icon.svg`: the author's own Apex Desktop flame and lightning mark, identical to ApexDesktop/Assets/Apex.svg without its plate.
- The "under 0.1% CPU" card copy comes from a 30-second idle measurement on Home (0.03% of total CPU, down from 4.35% before the monitoring rework).

## Tape 31 showcase

- `tape31/title.webp`, `tape31/corridor.webp`, `tape31/terminal.webp`, `tape31/chamber.webp`, `tape31/facility.webp`: in-game and title screen captures from Tape 31, a psychological found-footage horror game created for the Game Theory Fall Jam by Eugen Ranow and co-developer; encoded in WebP for web delivery. Playable on itch.io (https://eugenranow.itch.io/tape-31).
